      How it works

      Cheap checks on everything, expensive judgement only where it's needed.

      Stage 1 · every chunk

      Triage

      Re-checks access against the live identity provider, not the copy in the index, and scores each chunk for injection and poisoning. No LLM, sub-millisecond.

      Stage 2 · only what looks risky

      Investigate

      Ranks the suspicious chunks and spends a fixed LLM budget on the worst ones. Blatant attacks are blocked for free; if the model is down, rules decide.

      Stage 3 · what the model may see

      Release

      Redacts secrets and personal data, then checks whether the surviving chunks combine into something above the user's clearance — and withholds the fewest needed.
